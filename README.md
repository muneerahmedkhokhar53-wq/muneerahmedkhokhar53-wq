## About

I'm a Mechatronics Engineering student at **Mehran University of Engineering & Technology (MUET), Jamshoro, Pakistan**, interested in building intelligent systems that connect software with the physical world.

My focus is **robotics, computer vision, machine learning, embedded systems, and automation**. I enjoy developing simulations and practical prototypes, learning through experiments, and documenting engineering decisions clearly.

- **Robotics:** kinematics, robot simulation, teleoperation, and control concepts
- **Computer vision:** OpenCV, MediaPipe, face analysis, and vision-based interaction
- **AI engineering:** Python, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and multi-agent workflows
- **Embedded systems:** Arduino-based prototyping and hardware/software integration
- **CAD & design:** mechanical design and mechatronic system development

## How I approach projects

- Build a small, testable version before adding complexity.
- Document installation, configuration, controls, and known limitations.
- Prefer reproducible experiments and measured results over unsupported performance claims.
- Treat privacy and safe operation as design requirements, especially for camera-based systems.
